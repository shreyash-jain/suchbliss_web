Founder Story

Shuchita Srivastava is the Proud Mother of two C-section born children and a heart and soul behind Suchbliss, a visionary wellness coach who turned her personal healing into a purpose-driven mission to empower women worldwide.

Her journey began much like many others, juggling work, family, and endless responsibilities while silently struggling with postpartum weight gain, hormonal imbalances, hypothyroidism, chronic back pain, severe migraines, menstrual issues, low iron levels, and constant fatigue.

When her body began to show signs of distress, she decided to take charge of her health instead of giving in to the challenges.

She turned to Yoga, Strength Training, and Nutrition, and through consistent, mindful practice, she healed her body, balanced her hormones completely and reclaimed her energy and confidence. Today, at the age of 44, she proudly lives a medicine-free life, stronger, healthier, and more vibrant than she was in her late 20s and early 30s.

That transformation sparked a deeper calling, to help other women experience the same freedom and power she found within herself. As a woman, wife, mother, and working professional, Shuchita deeply understands the physical and emotional needs and challenges women face, especially in their 30s, 40s, 50s and beyond.

With over 11 years of experience in Yoga and Fitness, and professional certifications as a Yoga Teacher, Fitness Trainer, and Nutrition Coach, Shuchita has guided thousands of women on their healing journeys. Her holistic and compassionate approach focuses not just on physical results, but on building emotional resilience, mental clarity, and self-love.

She has been recognized with multiple awards and honours in the field of Health and Wellness, but her true reward lies in the success stories of her students, women who have overcome disease, rediscovered their strength, and found peace through her guidance.

Today, Shuchita continues to inspire through her live events, workshops, online sessions, and vibrant wellness community. Her goal is simple yet profound:

To help every woman live free from pain, fatigue, and fear, and thrive with strength, grace, and bliss.
