The Platform is designed to support women aged 18 years and above (and women 35+ in particular) in managing hormonal health, including but not limited to PCOS, thyroid conditions, perimenopause, postpartum recovery, weight management, and general fitness and wellbeing, through structured yoga, strength training, pranayama, meditation and nutritional guidance.
